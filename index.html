Welcome to Miniature Studio 🌸

Craft your dream cozy room diorama in this 3D designer! Inspired by wooden craft kits like the Alice Tea Store, this playground lets you place miniature furniture, lights, and decor.

1

Choose a Block: Select any item from the left library. Hover over the wooden table to see a preview.

2

Place & Stack: Click to place a block. You can stack objects on top of tables, bookshelves, or floors.

3

Rotate & Adjust: Press R before or after placing to rotate. Click on placed items to customize their colors and textures.

4

Cozy Lighting: Place glowing lamps and toggle **Night Mode** (☀️/🌙 button) to turn on the warm LED light glow.
